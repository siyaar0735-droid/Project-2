The story behind Oud & Flower

Our story

Perfume and flowers, one love

Oud & Flower began with a simple idea: the best gifts are the ones you can smell and see. We bring together rich oud perfumes and fresh hand-made bouquets in one place, so every gift feels personal.


From weddings and birthdays to Eid and Valentine's Day, our team prepares each order with care, wrapped in a beautiful box, ready to make someone smile.
